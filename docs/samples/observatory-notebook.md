# Observatory field notes

## Opening

The North Ridge Observatory opened in 1998. The site was selected for its dry climate and low artificial-light levels. Its first public night welcomed 240 visitors.

## Instruments

The primary telescope uses a 4.2-meter mirror. A smaller 0.8-meter telescope is reserved for student projects. The observatory added a solar spectrograph in 2012.

## Research program

The research team monitors variable stars and near-Earth asteroids. Public observing sessions take place on the first Friday of each month, weather permitting.

## Visitor information

The visitor center opens at 6 p.m. during public nights. Visitors should bring a warm layer because temperatures fall after sunset. The center provides printed sky maps at no charge.
